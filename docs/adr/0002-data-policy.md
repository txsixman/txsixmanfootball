# ADR 0002: Permission-first data

**Status:** Accepted, Nov 10, 2026 (the owner). Still needs a Texas attorney's review before launch (plan 7.1).

## Decision
Collect facts first-hand, cite secondary sources, and use anyone else's compilation only with permission.

| Source | Use |
|---|---|
| Reporters, coaches, fans | Primary |
| UIL and private-league public documents | Alignments, brackets, official dates |
| School and booster posts | A human checks a submission against them |
| Newspaper archives | Historical facts, cited |
| SixManFootball.com | Only through partnership. Never scraped |
| MaxPreps | Manual check of one disputed game. Its terms bar scraping |
| Dave Campbell's | Mention a rank with credit. Never republish polls |
| CalPreps | Not used |

## Why
Game results are facts, but a compiled database, editorial rankings and narratives are someone's work and are often
governed by site terms. Staying clean also keeps the partnership path open.

## Consequences
History is slow to build, and multi-season game data must be gathered by us (`v1.2-notes.md`, item 3).
