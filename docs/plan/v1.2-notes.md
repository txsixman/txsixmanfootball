# Corrections for plan v1.2

From a read of plan v1.1. Internal contradictions and arithmetic were checked against the plan itself. Outside facts
(court rulings, prices, UIL rules) were **not** re-verified. The hour totals, the 42-week capacity figure and the worked confidence example all add up.

## Must resolve

1. **The quiet build is not quiet.** Section 15.3 says build quietly and approach the owner in June-July 2027. But the
   plan ships a public SEO alpha in Feb-Apr 2027, and invites its founder and its leading record-keeper in Feb 2027 (sections 7.5, 14, 17).
   He will hear about it from them first, which is the "seen as hostile" risk in section 20.
   **Proposed:** talk to the owner before the historian invitations and before the public alpha.

2. **The name conflicts with the plan.** Section 15 bars using the site's name without permission, and section 13.1
   defers the brand until after the partnership talk. `txsixmanfootball.com` is one prefix away from
   `sixmanfootball.com`. **Proposed:** keep it parked and off all public use until Decision 2, and include it in the
   trademark check. Separately, 13.1 and the Phase 2 exit choose a brand by Feb-Apr 2027, before the June talk.

3. **Gate G3 cannot pass as written.** It requires ratings to beat both baselines on a backtest by Apr 18, 2027. The
   plan has only the 2026 playoffs captured by then, plus whatever a spring drive finds. H2 is season-level and H3 is
   game-level "ongoing". Backtesting and fitting the prior (rho) need several seasons of games, and the sites that have
   them are off limits. **Proposed:** make 2026 regular-season capture a tracked epic with its own gate, name which
   back seasons get game-level capture, and restate G3 for the data that will exist.

4. **The 482 hours cover building only.** Not budgeted: recruiting reporters and moderators, ADs,
   the LLC, the attorney, sponsors, and the partnership pitch. At 11.5 hours a week the build alone uses every hour
   (at 10 a week it is 420). The real pre-launch position is negative. **Proposed:** add these hours and pre-apply the
   first cuts from section 5.2.

## Design bugs

5. **Network dedupe will undercount real fans (7.3).** Distinct submitters are deduplicated by account, device and
   network. At a rural stadium many honest fans share one carrier gateway or school wifi, so they collapse into one.
   **Proposed:** dedupe on account and device; use network only as a fraud signal.

6. **The rating formula covers one direction (8.2).** It writes only `P(m_g >= 45)`, a home blowout. An away win by 45
   needs `P(m_g <= -45)`. Fix before the notebook, along with old games whose ending is unknown (ADR 0003).

7. **15-second freshness conflicts with stale-while-revalidate (6.1, 9.4).** With a 10-15 second TTL, a stale copy can
   be served past the 15-second target, longest on quiet pages. Define the target at the cache, or shorten the TTL.

8. **Cloudflare should not proxy in front of Vercel (Figure 3, section 9.4).** Vercel's docs advise against it, so
   ADR 0001 uses Cloudflare DNS-only and caches scoreboards on Vercel's CDN. Redraw Figure 3.

## Found while building the ratings prototype

- Section 8.2 needs a prior on sigma. A bracket has about one game per team, can be fit perfectly, and sigma collapses.
- Projected margins above 45 should display as "45+". A final cannot show more, so a bare "62" promises what cannot happen.
- On simulated seasons, censoring recovers true team strength better but barely changes game predictions. The case for
  it is fair ratings of dominant teams; do not promise better picks.
