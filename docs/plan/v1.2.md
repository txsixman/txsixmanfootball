# Plan v1.2

Nov 11, 2026. Plan v1.1 (a PDF kept outside this repo) with the changes below; where they differ, this
version wins. Source: `v1.2-notes.md`, answered by the owner on Nov 11, 2026, and ADR 0014.

## 1. Who builds it (notes item 4; ADR 0014)
The owner, alone, with Claude Code. Out of the plan, with their hours:
- reporters, moderators, athletic directors and historians (v1.1 sections 1, 4, 7.5, 14, 17)
- the LLC, the attorney, sponsors and the partnership pitch (sections 11, 13, 15, 20)

Data comes from public internet sources under the data ADRs (0002, 0005, 0006, 0008 to 0013). The 482 hours are
build hours only, which is now all the work there is.

## 2. The incumbent site (notes item 1; ADR 0014)
No owner talk, before or after launch. Sections 15.3 ("approach the owner in June-July 2027") and 20's partnership
risks are void. The hard lines in section 15 stay as conduct rules: never read the incumbent's site, never poach its
people, never use its name or marks.

## 3. The name (notes item 2; ADR 0014)
`txsixmanfootball.com` is the site's public address once the owner connects it (DNS on his Cloudflare account; Vercel
project `txsixmanfootball.com`). Section 13.1's later brand choice is dropped. The site stays `noindex` until the owner
opens it.

## 4. Gate G3, restated (notes item 3)
v1.1: ratings beat both baselines on a backtest by Apr 18, 2027, which needed seasons of games the plan had no way to
get. The data now exists: every game of the 2021 to 2026 seasons read from MaxPreps schedule pages (ADR 0008).

**G3, Apr 18, 2027:** on a walk-forward backtest over the 2021 to 2026 seasons (each week rated only from games
before it), the ratings beat both v1.1 baselines on the games of every season from 2022 on. The prior (rho) is fit on
2021 to 2024 and checked on 2025 and 2026. Regular-season capture is done and kept current by the Results watch, so
it is no longer an open epic.

## 5. Phases that change
- **Phase 1 (data core)** keeps `phase-1-backlog.md`, less item 2's outreach: TCAL, TCSAAL and independents are added
  only from public pages.
- **Phase 3 (game night)**: no reporter app, no moderator queues, no SMS entry. Scores come from the robot reads and
  push alerts; the admin console (Phase 1 item 6) corrects them. Its backlog is written when Phase 2 ends, and its
  hours are recounted then.
- **Gate G5**: no attorney sign-off. `legal-checklist.md` stays as a reference.

## 6. Design fixes from the notes (applied as proposed)
- **5. Network dedupe:** moot with no crowd submissions. If submissions ever return: dedupe on account and device,
  network only as a fraud signal.
- **6. Both blowout directions:** the rating model writes `P(m_g >= 45)` and `P(m_g <= -45)`; old games whose ending
  is unknown are censored (ADR 0003).
- **7. Freshness:** the 15-second target is measured at the cache, not the origin.
- **8. Cloudflare is DNS-only** in front of Vercel (ADR 0001); Figure 3 is read that way.
- **From the ratings prototype:** a prior on sigma; projected margins above 45 show as "45+"; censoring is for fair
  ratings of dominant teams, not better picks.
